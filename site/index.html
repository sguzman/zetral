Research question

H ψₙ = γₙ ψₙ

Can arithmetic define a meaningful spectral system whose eigenvalues encode the nontrivial zeta zeros? Our first campaign investigates the quantitative convergence of spectral approximations constructed using Weil's quadratic form.

Candidate result

0 ≤ ta,n − γn ≤ Kn X5/2 e−2πX

For fixed n and sufficiently large a, with X = e2a, under RH and Suzuki's sampling and inverse-compression results. The argument combines a zero-isolating theta kernel, compact truncation and a quadratic Ritz estimate. Five supporting scalar lemmas passed Lean verification; the full analytic and spectral proof remains unformalized and independently unreviewed. Explicit zero-dependent constants are given in manuscript v0.2.
