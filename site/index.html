Research question

For fixed n and sufficiently large a, with X = e2a, under RH and Suzuki's sampling and inverse-compression results. Small-window asymptotics

a ta,n = (n − ½)π + dn/ℓ + qn/ℓ² + on(ℓ⁻²)

For fixed n as a decreases to zero, ℓ = log(1/a). The first correction dn = Si((2n−1)π)/2 is strictly positive; the second has an explicit convergent integral. The local candidate derivation does not assume RH, unlike the global large-window comparison. The proof uses Suzuki's unconditional local extension theorem and direct prime-free coercivity. The second-order coefficient evaluations and worker finite spectra are non-interval-certified diagnostics; independent human review and priority remain outstanding.

Uniform phase spectrum

a ta,θ,n = rn + [Si(2rn) − sin(2rn)]/(2ℓ) + On(ℓ⁻²)

For fixed positive index n and ℓ = log(1/a), the estimate is uniform over all natural deficiency phases. The effective angle is arg(cos(θ/2) + ia sin(θ/2)); the exact zero at phase 0 is excluded from positive indexing. The local candidate derivation assumes no RH. A separate bounded-branch estimate resolves the central eigenvalue. Independent human review and priority remain outstanding.

Arbitrary-phase large-window spectrum

Γ ⊎ {tan(θ/2)}, θ ≠ π

The limiting signed local spectral multiset includes one additional phase-dependent eigenvalue, with limiting multiplicity two at a collision. Phase π has no extra finite mode. The argument uses Suzuki's distinguished-phase compression, a rank-one inverse identity and weak escape of the normalized evaluation vector. A scoped independent AI audit of Campaign VII was accepted; no human review, proof of RH or established originality.

Normalized kernel singularity

Ja(w,z)/Ja(0,0) → ΔΓ(w)̅ ΔΓ(z)

On fixed complex compacts the leading kernel has rank one. At a sampled ordinate γ the diagonal retains the finite value 1/mγ; offsets of order 1/√Ja(0,0) give a quadratic microscopic profile. The common entire phase characteristic tends to [z cos(θ/2) − sin(θ/2)] ΔΓ(z). These candidate deductions assume RH; the fixed-separation mixed kernel remains unresolved and the independent audit is pending.
