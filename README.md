# Zetral

**Spectral mathematics and the Riemann zeta function**

Zetral is an open research project exploring the Hilbert–Pólya program: the search for a self-adjoint operator whose spectrum encodes the nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function.

The project studies localized arithmetic forms, differentiation operators, and their spectral limits, with an emphasis on quantitative estimates and falsifiable mathematical conjectures.

## Research integrity

Zetral is ongoing AI-assisted mathematical research under human direction. Candidate proofs are distinguished from imported theorems, conditional results, numerical evidence, and machine-checked lemmas. Independent review and originality assessment remain outstanding. **No proof of the Riemann hypothesis is claimed.**
