# Zetral

A Spectral Mathematics Research Project.

Open research into the spectral, arithmetic and dynamical structures underlying the Riemann zeta function, organized around the Hilbert–Pólya program.

**Status:** Campaign I concluded with explicit blockers. Campaign II (rigorous verification and novelty assessment) is concluded with explicit numerical-certification and infrastructure blockers. Campaign III (sharpness and discovery) concluded with a stronger candidate upper bound and strict finite-cutoff separation; matching sharpness remains unresolved. Campaign IV (exact-local spectral certification) concluded with a conditional residual framework and analytic small-window example; practical numerical certification remains outstanding. Campaign V (small-window spectral asymptotics) concluded with an unconditional candidate two-term correction for the phase-pi differentiation spectrum. Campaign VI (phase-dependent small-window spectrum) is active under standing authorization for autonomous mathematical research. A conditional fixed-index convergence bound is presented as a self-reviewed candidate proof; novelty and independent verification remain outstanding. AI-assisted research with human direction.
