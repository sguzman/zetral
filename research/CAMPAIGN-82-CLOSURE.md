# Campaign LXXXII closure — packet liminf phase criterion

Date: 2026-11-27. Status: CLOSED within the structural-criterion scope.

## Question and acceptance

Campaign LXXXII asked what determines the liminf of the Weil values on fixed
paired edge packets, with no unproved assumptions on zero ordinates.
Acceptance allowed an exact phase-orbit characterization plus a conditional
zero-liminf statement with its hypotheses explicit.

## Finding

B1-16 proves that the liminf is zero exactly when the target phases that
cancel every spectral summand lie in the closure of the one-parameter torus
flow generated by the signed zero ordinates. Finite projections of this
closure are characterized by the exact integer-relation lattice, and the
infinite zero tail is controlled uniformly by an explicit Schwartz tail.
An incompatible exact relation forces a strictly positive liminf. If the
positive ordinates are Q-linearly independent, Kronecker's theorem implies
zero liminf conditionally.

The literature audit found the positive-ordinate linear-independence
statement is the classical LI conjecture; Lamzouri's effective strengthening
is also explicitly conjectural. No exact nontrivial relation or independence
claim was established. Thus the actual liminf remains undetermined by this
campaign.

## Verification and limits

The orbit-closure criterion is a manual derivation from Suzuki's spectral
formula, compact-group character theory, and uniform tail convergence. It
has not been formalized. It is specific to fixed profiles and proves no
full-space coercivity. The actual arithmetic phase-compatibility question is
open; its dependence on the classical LI conjecture is recorded rather than
assumed.

## Pivot

Campaign LXXXIII investigates a different analytical route: uniform
boundedness of the first-prime endpoint dual multiplier is equivalent to RH
by a Laplace-transform pole argument. See `research/CAMPAIGN-83.md` and
B1-17. The new route does not require ordinate independence.
