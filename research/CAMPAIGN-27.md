# Campaign XXVII — Twisted prime phase-limit spectrum

Opened 2026-11-25 under standing authorization after WPR04 (a452e5436a51feda0c9cb9fb7f53733c608a8a80). The current question is the largest-scale ordinary negative depth, not a new generalized metric and not merely a near-saturation necessary condition. WPR01–03 show that smooth PNT approximations alone cannot resolve the high-carrier arithmetic phase obstruction.

- TPH01: construct the compact half-line Hankel models for vague twisted prime-measure limits under modulation. Prove domination by the untwisted exponential density, compactness of each model, fixed-envelope convergence of the modulated prime blocks, and the ordinary spectral lower bound after the carrier log cost and pole are included.
- TPH02: test the converse for the bottom ordinary depth. Use the FES01 exact partition with fixed support expansion h and an adjustable moment penalty to retain arbitrarily close to the original Rayleigh depth. Prove compact local convergence of demodulated endpoint envelopes, control escaped mass by uniform endpoint-tail bounds, and handle bounded carriers and the pole separately. Take subsequential limits before h and penalty tend to zero. If justified, obtain an exact characterization of limsup exp(-a)N_1(a) by the supremum of the attainable twisted Hankel norm minus carrier cost. Otherwise record the precise failure.
- TPH03: analyze spectral and arithmetic consequences of the phase-limit model, including the condition for norm one and any rigorously justified higher-index lower bounds. Do not infer an upper bound on higher indices from a bottom-only packet selection. Determine which conclusions genuinely improve WPR's necessary coherence condition.
- TPH04: audit, consolidate and select a productive next direction. Distinguish an exact characterization from computing the attainable phase-limit set or proving a smaller exponent.
