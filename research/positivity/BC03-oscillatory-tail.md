## Bound
In the inherited high-mode expansion the common numerator coefficient of S_(2j)/n^(2j+1) is L(n)=A(n)+sum_s p_s sin(n theta_s), theta_s=pi*s/h, p_s=2sqrt(2)b_s/pi, A(n)=2sqrt(2)n F[n]/h. The inherited bound |A(n)|<=a=sqrt(2)/2+(2sqrt(2)h/pi^2)(1+E_h)/(M+1) follows from the same rate sum estimate as CL=a+sum p_s; no changes to remainder C8. For each power p=2,6,10,14, set w_n=n^(-p)/g(n) over one parity, n>M. Let H_p=1/(2(p-1)(M-1)^(p-1)g(M+1)), and R_p the previously certified geometric upper factor for sum w_n/H_p. Since g positive increasing, w_n decreases to zero. Geometric partial sums of exp(i n theta) on either parity have modulus <=1/|sin theta| if nonzero. Abel summation gives |sum w_n exp(i n theta)|<=w_first/|sin theta|. Also w_first/H_p<=2(p-1)/(M+1), and triangle gives <=R_p. Thus U(theta)=min(R_p,2(p-1)/((M+1)|sin theta|)) is an upper bound after dividing by H_p; at a zero or uncertified denominator use R_p. Expand the sine square via product-to-sum: the weighted oscillatory square divided by H_p is bounded by V=1/2 sum_s p_s^2 (R_p+U(2theta_s))+sum_{s<t}p_s p_t(U(theta_s-theta_t)+U(theta_s+theta_t)). Weighted Minkowski yields sum w_n L(n)^2/H_p <= (a sqrt(R_p)+sqrt(V))^2. Hence replace the existing CL^2 H_p rank bound by min(R_p,(a sqrt(R_p)+sqrt(V))^2/CL^2) times CL^2 H_p. Applies only to the common oscillatory ranks 1,3,5,7; constant numerator ranks and eighth remainder use previous geometric factors. Positive rank rescaling is valid in Loewner order, not an entrywise inequality. Outward scalar upper bounds and signed interval scaling are used in exact replay.
