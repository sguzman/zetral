## 1. Audit of the fixed construction

The nonnegative Schwartz envelope is the square of the Fourier transform of a real even nonnegative compact smooth function; its transform is nonnegative and supported in a fixed finite interval. Its imaginary-shift average has transform cosh(bv)Fp(v). The lacunary domination estimate follows by splitting centers below half the argument, within a factor of two, and above twice the argument. It proves local logarithmic density and permits choosing a common coefficient that keeps the real baseline density strictly positive. The total off-line mass through T is O(log T) because L_l=log T_l grows geometrically; centers beyond 2T contribute a uniformly bounded integrated Schwartz tail. The absolute total real density correction has the same cumulative estimate. Thus the combined positive-ordinate count has the Riemann-von Mangoldt main term and O(log T) error. These are count shapes, not an identity of zeta counts.

The nonoscillatory off-line analytic sampling cancels its subtracted real projection exactly by the transform cosh factor. The remaining cosine envelopes have compact physical difference-frequency bands about +/-k_l. For any fixed support window only finitely many active bands occur; the autocorrelation norm is bounded by ||f||^2 and each band contributes at most C L_l exp(b k_l)=O(exp(s a_l)). The fixed spacing of a_l bounds their active sum by O(exp(s a)). Thus the ordinary log reference form plus bounded perturbation is established without assuming a small absolute population.

## 2. Precise logical obstruction

For any fixed 0<b<1/2 and any s in (2b,1], the construction supplies a single fixed discrete symmetric abstract zero system with:
- only simple integer-multiplicity nodes, at deviations zero or b;
- Riemann-von Mangoldt leading count with O(log T) error and local O(log T) count;
- globally only O(log T) nonreal nodes, hence strong global rarity;
- ordinary closed semibounded log forms with compact resolvent at every finite window and compatible support inclusion;
- fixed-support high-carrier paired normalization, exact positive strip projection of every normalized local limit, and the fractional signed projection discrepancy bound of LZC02;
- a symmetric real entire function of order one realizing the nodes;
- ordinary negative bottom depth comparable to exp(sa), with s strictly above the geometric rate 2b.

These properties do not, alone or jointly, imply the ordinary matching upper exponent in the abstract class. In particular global zero density, symmetry, entire order and local analytic projection identities cannot simply replace a missing arithmetic argument. The model lacks the actual atomic prime-power distribution with the specified von Mangoldt coefficients, the exact zeta Euler product and the gamma-factor/Dirichlet-series functional relation. It is not known to satisfy the actual arithmetic prime-side estimates beyond the explicitly matched ordinary coarse and signed projection bounds. No zeta anomaly is produced.
