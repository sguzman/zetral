Indeed choose the fixed smooth endpoint approximants as close as desired in L2 to the limiting exponential columns; then the finite cross Gram inverse converges to the orthogonal Gram inverse up to the same arbitrarily small perturbation.

Consequently the map from the orthogonal column-span/kernel decomposition into the form domain, replacing W_a^0 by W_a, is an arbitrarily near-isometry in the ordinary metric. Its finite selected form is unchanged, while the residual mixed terms and finite residual block are ordinary-norm bounded by the FZE02 estimate, with a constant depending on the approximation accuracy but not a.
