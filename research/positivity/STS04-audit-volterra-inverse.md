## 1. Audit of the trace and its inverse

STS01's evaluation norm bound follows by applying Cauchy-Schwarz to f and f' separately and adding their inequalities using F(f')=-iz F(f); the zero extension has vanishing endpoint traces. Summability of the rank-one trace norms uses the unconditional local O(log(2+|u|)) count and the quadratic high-ordinate denominator. Reflection sends k_z to k_-z, so the trace contribution is F[k_(-conj z)](z), with no extra conjugate in the Green integral. The elementary ODE solution satisfies both Dirichlet endpoints. Its integrals against exp(izx) produce the stated Theta_z, including the removable z=0 check 2a-2tanh a. All actual ordinates avoid ±i and 0.

STS02's exponential coefficients simplify using i(1-z^2)-2z=i(1+iz)^2; the sign and doubling under z->-z give lambda_z=-2iz=1-2rho and C_z=i/[z(1-iz)^2]=2/[lambda_z(1+lambda_z/2)^2]. The coefficients are nonzero and absolutely summable with multiplicity. The residual Dirichlet corrections are O(exp(-2a)); their Laplace transform is analytic on Re s>-2. The pole series converges normally away from its discrete poles there. Consequently the simple poles and their residues identify the complete zero multiset from exact full parity traces. Multiplicities occur in residues, not higher pole orders. The RH boundedness equivalence and exact limsup exponent follow from the upper exponential bound and analytic contradiction at a pole to the right of any hypothesized smaller growth exponent. They do not give a lower bound at every window or prove boundedness.

STS03 derives the same trace from the arithmetic form by the Green expansion, with the nonnegative digamma difference forms separately summed on each parity before subtraction. The reflected shift trace K_a(s) is even and C^1 across its support endpoints, with derivative -(L-s)sinh(s)/(2 sinh L) on 0<s<L. In particular the prime weight at n=exp L vanishes. The pole factor 4 int cosh(s/2)K_a(s) is consistent with the source's 2cosh kernel. Archimedean summands are nonnegative and the bound L coth L/[16(J-1)^2] follows from the r_j^-3 summation. The prime-inclusive exact rational test at a=log 2 yielded P in [-288912938,-263181818]/10^12, both negative; this is a signed parity trace, not a negative ordinary or Sobolev eigenvalue certificate. ## 2. Exact Volterra relation to the screw function

Let g be the real even continuous screw function in Suzuki (1.3), with g(0)=0. The arithmetic difference kernel is the distribution -g'' as in (2.9)–(2.11). By STS03's trace formula, its pairing with the reflected Green correlation K_a is P(a). This extends from smooth test kernels to K_a: it is compactly supported C^1 with piecewise bounded second derivative, and smoothing it converges in C^1 and in the L1 norm of second derivatives. The explicit arithmetic distribution pairing is continuous under this approximation: near zero use |K(0)-K(s)|<=C|s| and k(s)=O(1/s), away from zero use uniform convergence with bounded support, and the finite prime evaluations and pole integral are continuous. Hence
    P(a)=<-g'',K_a>=-int_R g(s)K_a''(s)ds.
There are no additional endpoint delta terms in K_a'' because K_a and K_a' are continuous at ±L. The source g is even; using the derivative of K_a from STS03 for L=2a gives
    J(L):=sinh L P(L/2)
         =int_0^L g(s)[(L-s)cosh s-sinh s]ds.            (1)
This is an exact elementary Volterra transform, not an asymptotic statement. Since g is continuous, J is C^1, with
    J'(L)=int_0^L g(s)cosh s ds-g(L)sinh L.
In distributions on L>0,
    J''(L)=-sinh L g'(L).                               (2)
The cancellation of the two g(L)cosh L terms is exact. The explicit g is smooth away from the locally finite prime-power thresholds and zero (it is locally absolutely continuous on L>0). Thus exact knowledge of P on the positive half-line recovers g' by (2) and g by its known limit g(0)=0, with integration interpreted on L>0 and the limit at zero. At L=log n, n a prime power, the jump in g' is Lambda(n)/sqrt(n) from the ramp term of (1.3); hence the corresponding jump in J'' is -sinh(log n) Lambda(n)/sqrt(n). This reconstructs prime-power weights from the trace in exact data. No differentiation of noisy measured trace data is justified.

This identity supplies an additional check on the arithmetic and spectral signs and clarifies information content: the scalar parity trace is an invertible smoothing of the classical screw function, while STS03's large-window simplification is the smoothed prime sum with Mellin kernel 1/[s(s+1)^2]. Consequently the global zero recovery/RH criterion of STS02 should be understood as an operator realization of classical explicit-formula information, not an independent discovery of zero locations or a new proof mechanism by itself. No claim that bounding derivatives of (1) from bounded P is easy.
