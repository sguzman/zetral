# MVI04 — Moment visibility audit and finite arithmetic evaluation direction

2026-11-25. Scoped AI lead audit, not independent human/formal review. No actual negative ordinary zeta eigenvalue, numerical heat matrix, RH proof or priority claim.

MVI01's scalar spectral measure has an exponential moment by APM01. Thus polynomial density in the weighted form norm follows from APM03, and each visible eigenvalue contributes exactly one dimension even if its full multiplicity is larger. The negative index of the shifted Hankel matrix exhausts the visible count for a fixed threshold; it does not give a finite degree for zero count. The triangular rank-one power identity preserves the finite Krylov spaces and hence the reducing cyclic subspace. On that subspace a common eigenvalue of T and A would force the A eigenvector orthogonal to u by projection onto the visible T eigenspace, which is impossible. Common invisible modes remain exactly unchanged and cannot be inferred from the relative trace.

MVI02 extends form density columnwise, so the block moment inertia counts rank(E_lambda V) at each distinct eigenvalue rather than one. The CPC04 splitting uses s=-z and weights (mu+z-S_chi)_+; the finite arithmetic seeds are weighted restricted Fourier modes, with parity projection if needed. On the entire negative spectral subspace V* is bounded below by sqrt(mu) since B-z=P-VV*, P>=mu. This is a simultaneous bound on combinations across eigenvalues and therefore gives complete multiplicity visibility. It does not assert cyclicity above z. The dense sine basis alternative is ordinary Galerkin approximation.

MVI03 uses the first j eigenvectors for the lower heat-matrix bound, and removes the first j-1 rank-one contributions for the upper bound. This handles repeated eigenvalues without a gap between them. For j>r the upper bound is W, and the strict threshold crossing only certifies negative eigenvalues; zero eigenvalues never require a crossing. For j<=r the two-sided depth interval has width log(W/mu)/t. A certified numerical interval may be used only with the stated endpoint direction and, for the logarithm of its lower endpoint, positivity. The Laguerre generating function follows by the binomial identity; the Cauchy estimate on its coefficients uses q<rho<1 and the real-part minimum -rho/(1+rho). The exponential-moment constant bounds the integrated error in operator norm via the positive spectral matrix trace. For any fixed positive t the geometric degree error tends to zero, but no uniform rate as t grows is claimed. The method requires exact or certified matrix moments and a certified exponential-moment constant, which have not been evaluated for the actual arithmetic operator.
