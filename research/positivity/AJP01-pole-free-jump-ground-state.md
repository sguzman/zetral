# AJP01 — Pole-free arithmetic jump operator and ground state

2026-11-25. Candidate AI derivation, unconditional in RH; not independently verified. Prior-art overlap identified this turn: https://zeta.teal-sea.com/read/hunts/weil_propagation/theory/RESULTS.html (Proposition M, lines 126–134), an explicitly unreviewed research note, already states pole-free positivity improvement, simplicity and evenness. Do not claim novelty for these facts. Suzuki arXiv:2606.09096v4 HTML §5.2 (lines 676ff) uses the analogous Dirichlet argument for the archimedean part. GSG03, STS03 and PBC01 give the exact normalization and PNT limit.

Let I=(-a,a), L=2a, all functions extended by zero, c_n=Lambda(n)/sqrt(n), s_n=log n, P=sum_(s_n<L)c_n(T_s+T_-s). Set alpha0=psi(1/4)-log pi and k(s)=exp(-s/2)/(1-exp(-2s)). By STS03 the archimedean form is alpha0||f||²+int_0^infty k(s)||T_s f-f||²_(L²(R)) ds. Thus T=A-H has form
    t(f)=(alpha0-2 sum c_n)||f||²+E(f),
    E(f)=int_0^infty k(s)||tau_s f-f||² ds
         +sum c_n||tau_s f-f||².
Here tau is full-line translation, not a compressed shift in the squared norm. In particular exit jumps are not discarded. The finite atom term is bounded on L²; the closed domain is the ordinary zero-extended log form domain inherited from GSG03, and the resolvent is compact. Absolute values and real normal contractions decrease E, by pointwise differences on the full line; closure on the zero-extended domain follows from the log-energy characterization (equivalently approximation by inward dilation and mollification). This is a killed symmetric jump form plus a scalar.

For an elementary strict positivity proof, put j0=k(L)>0 (k decreases) and J=1 tensor 1 on I. The continuous part is (1/2)int_R² k(|x-y|)|f(x)-f(y)|² dxdy. Subtract j0 on I×I, leaving a nonnegative jump kernel and the same exit killing. The removed part is j0(L||f||²-|int_I f|²). Hence T=S-j0 J, where S has a real scalar plus a closed Markovian jump form. The bounded subtraction changes neither domain nor compactness. The resolvent minimization for S shifted sufficiently positive shows its resolvent is positivity preserving (replace minimizer by its absolute value), and the Euler semigroup limit does likewise. For f,g>=0 nonzero, the positive Dyson expansion gives
    <g,e^(-tT) f> >= j0 int_0^t <g,e^(-(t-r)S)1><1,e^(-rS)f> dr >0.
Indeed each semigroup image of a nonzero nonnegative vector is nonnegative and nonzero by injectivity. Thus the semigroup is positivity improving for every t>0. Its compactness follows from compact resolvent. Taking absolute value of a real ground eigenvector does not raise energy, so a nonnegative ground state exists and is strictly positive a.e. If a real ground eigenvector were sign-changing, the strictly positive cross-kernel gives t(|f|)<t(f) (the difference is at least 4j0||f_+||_1||f_-||_1); impossible. Two orthogonal real ground states would therefore be impossible. Reflection commutes with T, so the normalized positive ground state phi is even and the lowest odd eigenvalue is strictly higher. This is NOT a statement about the full operator A=T+H.

Strict window comparison: for b<a, zero extension preserves all archimedean and prime correlations (extra shifts vanish), so t_a restricted to I_b equals t_b, including the changing scalar and exit terms together. Equality of lowest eigenvalues would extend a nonnegative ground state by zero to a minimizer on I_a, contrary to strict positivity. Hence mu_1(a)<mu_1(b). The translate argument of U01 also applies to the pole-free form without simplicity.

Large-window bottom: alpha(t)>=alpha0 and PBC01 proves e^-a||P||->1, so liminf e^-a mu_1>=-1. For any real nonnegative unit g in C_c^infty(0,infty), place g at both endpoints with coefficient 1/sqrt(2); for large a supports disjoint. PBC01 strong boundary limit gives e^-a <P f_a,f_a> -> |<g,h>|² with h(t)=exp(-t/2), ||h||=1. Archimedean expectation is bounded independently of a by the Fourier inequality |F f_a|²<=2|F g|² for real g (reflection and translation), using a fixed lower shift of alpha to nonnegative. Hence limsup e^-a mu_1<=-|<g,h>|². Approximate h by normalized nonnegative smooth compact g to conclude
    lim_(a->infty) e^-a mu_1(T_a)=-1.
For actual unit ground states, alpha expectation = mu_1+<P phi,phi> <= mu_1+||P||=o(e^a) and >=alpha0. Consequently the log Fourier moment is o(e^a) since alpha differs from the log weight by a bounded multiplier. This is not a subexponential bound and is only for the pole-free ground state. In particular no norm convergence of eigenvectors or estimate of the full ordinary negative exponent follows.
