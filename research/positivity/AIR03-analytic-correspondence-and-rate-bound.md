## Independent Euler–Maclaurin proof and sharper tail

Instead of relying on the chain of corrected midpoint quadratures, let A_d be the trapezoid sum f(R)/2+sum_(j>=1) f(R+jd) for d=1,2. The midpoint sum over R+1,R+3,... equals A_1-A_2. Assume the functions and derivatives required below decay at infinity and f^(8) is integrable (true of these rational rate functions for R>0). Apply DLMF 2.10.1 with m=4 to each scaled f(R+d x) and send the upper endpoint to infinity. The identity and periodic Bernoulli remainder were actually retrieved from https://dlmf.nist.gov/2.10, HTML lines 44–59. By DLMF 24.8.1 (https://dlmf.nist.gov/24.8, lines 40–41), the Fourier series implies |B_8(u)|<=|B_8(0)|=1/30 for 0<=u<=1. Therefore
    A_d = (1/d) int_R^infty f - sum_(k=1)^3 d^(2k-1) B_(2k)/(2k)! f^(2k-1)(R) + e_d,
    |e_d| <= [2 d^7/(30*8!)] int_R^infty |f^(8)|.
Subtracting gives the same coefficients +1/12, -7/720, +31/30240 as P7W03, with the smaller rigorous error constant
    2(1+2^7)/(30*8!) = 43/201600 < 1/320.
