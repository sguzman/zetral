## Audit

LZC01 normalizes shifted zero measures by log(2+|T|). The shifted local total count gives local bounded mass and a uniform logarithmic horizontal tail majorant for rapid strip tests. Every vague limit is translation bounded and symmetric in the imaginary coordinate. Fixed-envelope modulation of the actual arithmetic form contributes precisely the ordinary inner product after normalization, because the bounded part is fixed in the physical window and the log multiplier shift differs by at most log(1+|t|)+O(1). A compact approximate-identity correlation then extends the identity to all scalar compact smooth tests. This does not exchange limits with growing physical support. The positive kernel is the real part of the shifted sech density, with multiplier cosh(vs)/cosh(cs); the contour transform has base normalization 1/(2c). Sweeping the measure and testing against Fh cancels the denominator by using h/cosh(cs), so the projected measure has constant density 1/(2pi). Conversely that projection determines only the analytic tests, not the complex measure.

LZC02 uses exponential decay to extend the scalar explicit formula from compact tests to e^(-iTs)/cosh(cs) at c>1/2. The Fourier transform is 2pi Q_(c,0)(t-T), the prime coefficient becomes (2/pi) Re sum of alternating odd-line zeta logarithmic derivatives, and the pole contribution is 2 Qtilde_(c,1/2)(T). Finite deletion is subtracted with full multiplicities. The continued expression for c>b follows by real analyticity at each fixed T beyond the omitted ordinates: the remaining kernel denominators have no poles for Re c>b; the odd-line log derivative series is locally normally convergent in its far-right tail and has no pole along the real c interval at those heights. The meromorphic pole/removed kernels are used only off their poles, not claimed positive outside the strip. The elementary zero-free disk log bound uses a bounded logarithm at a fixed far-right center and the polynomial growth from Euler summation. Borel-Caratheodory and Cauchy differentiation yield O(log T) on fixed compact substrips. The Gaussian-damped rectangle maximum principle interpolates with the bounded absolutely convergent right boundary, yielding exponent theta_c=(1/2-c)/(1/2-b) up to arbitrary fixed epsilon. No uniform bound as c decreases to b is obtained. The exact cosh reconstruction and weighted Young bound give ordinary energy/negative-depth exponent 2c/(1-theta), which with theta_c cannot improve one.

LZC03's nonuniform positive example uses a small interior point mass and subtracts its positive projection from the outer uniform population; the kernel composition identity gives exact cancellation. The extreme-boundary measure inequality follows by letting c decrease to b in the positive convolution inequality, using the approximate identity property. It is a fixed-resolution coefficient bound, not a log exponent improvement. The oscillatory moving model has the exact scalar test identity on fixed supports once k_a exceeds them, but a growing-support trial picks up the term at separation k_a. Scaling by exp((1-2b)a) matches the projected discrepancy exponent and gives rate one in that model, not in zeta. The actual anomalous packet argument retains the archimedean carrier cost as well as the negative depth, uses demodulated H^m to bound the complex product by C A exp(2cA) times a rapid relative weight, and extracts a point of large signed discrepancy after controlling the far tail. The logarithmic derivative localization uses the finite set of odd lines with real part <=1 and a Gaussian-damped rectangle to transfer a large value on a right line to the first line. The necessary inequality max(s,kappa)<=2c+kappa theta_c still permits anomalies. No actual large values satisfying the required condition are exhibited.

## Next direction: a fixed discrete obstruction satisfying global count constraints

The moving model is not yet a single discrete zero system, so a stronger test is worthwhile. For fixed 0<b<1/2 and desired rate s in (2b,1], choose very sparse heights T_l with log T_l=exp((s-2b)a_l), and widely separated shifts k_l approximately 2a_l. Start from a continuous real-line counting density with the Riemann-von Mangoldt main term. At each +/-T_l add symmetric off-line densities of size log T_l times a nonnegative Schwartz envelope p(u-T_l) times 1+epsilon cos(k_l(u-T_l)), with compactly supported Fourier transform of p. Subtract the nonoscillatory analytic projection Re p(u-T_l+ib) from the real-line baseline, using a small common coefficient to preserve positivity. The nonoscillatory pair projection then cancels exactly; the remaining oscillatory kernel is supported in narrow bands about +/-k_l. With lacunary k_l only finitely many such bands affect each fixed physical window, and the total operator norm of their active pieces should be O(exp(sa)). Sparse T_l should ensure global off-line count O(log T), full Riemann-von Mangoldt count up to O(log T), and local count O(log T).

Discretize the real and upper nonreal positive densities separately by their quantiles, then reflect and conjugate. Each cumulative discrepancy is bounded by a constant, so integration by parts against the paired analytic sample product should bound the resulting ordinary form difference by O(a exp(2ba)). This is below the proposed rate s. At window a_l, modulate a two-endpoint trial to T_l; its autocorrelation selects exactly the lth high-shift band, and the opposite carrier contributes a negligible rapidly oscillating integral. This may give the matching lower rate s. Finally check whether the fixed discrete model also retains LZC02's fractional-log projected discrepancy bound for every fixed c>b, via the exponentially attenuated high-shift Fourier bands and the bounded quantile error of the smooth sweep.

If successful, this is a much stronger countermodel to using only known zero counts and local analytic projection constraints, and it would identify the genuinely missing arithmetic structure. It is not a proposed zeta zero set or a counterexample to RH. The construction is not yet proved in this audit.
