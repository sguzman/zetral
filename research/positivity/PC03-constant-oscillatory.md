## Bound

Use the notation of BC03-oscillatory-tail.md. The high-mode coefficient A(n)=2sqrt(2)n F[n]/h can be split into a0=sqrt(2)/2 and error |A(n)-a0|<=epsilon=(2sqrt(2)h/pi^2)(1+E_h)/(M+1). Indeed the unweighted rate sum for f(r)=1/(r^2+omega^2), sampled at r_j=1/2+2j, differs from half its integral over [0,infinity), pi/(4 omega), by at most f(0). Monotone integral comparison bounds the sample sum between half the integral from 1/2 and that integral plus f(1/2); the omitted initial integral /2 is <=f(0)/4. The exponential part of F[n] has either sign by parity and magnitude <=E_h f(0), so the stated error bound follows with omega=n pi/h. For the sine sum S(n)=sum p_s sin(n theta_s), weighted Abel bounds U(theta) and V from BC03 give, after division by H_p,

sum w_n (a0+S(n))^2/H_p <= a0^2 R_p+V+2a0 sum p_s U(theta_s)=B.

A second Minkowski bound treats only the small archimedean error: sum w_n L(n)^2/H_p <=(sqrt(B)+epsilon sqrt(R_p))^2. Use the smaller of this divided by CL^2 and R_p for the positive rank rescaling. Constant numerator ranks and the eighth remainder are unchanged. All assumptions and parity weight monotonicity are as in BC03. This is an internal analytic derivation; independent audit still due.
