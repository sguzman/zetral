# BFA02 — Explicit boundary-tail error and executed accelerated first moment

2026-11-25. AI-assisted candidate derivation with executed integer interval arithmetic, not independently reviewed. Fixed CME03 benchmark only: I=(-1/2,1/2), circle length 4, unit constant trial, sharp physical cutoff |s|<=1. No actual negative restored eigenvalue or full positivity certificate.

Write t_n=pi n/2, b=log 2/sqrt 2, theta0=pi/2, thetap=pi log 2/2. The exact first-moment tail is
    R_N=(4/pi²) sum_(n>N) (1-cos(n theta0)) S(t_n)/n².
For t>=1 write S(t)=log(t/(2pi))-2b cos(t log 2)+e(t). The same remainder bound works for T and A:
    |e(t)| <= 2/t²+10/t.
Indeed DLMF 5.11.2 and §5.11(ii) (actual HTML retrieved this turn, https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.11#ii, line 239) bound the remainder of psi(z)=log z-1/(2z)+R at z=1/4+it/2 by sec^3(arg(z)/2)/(12 |z|²)<=2sqrt(2)/(3t²). Also log|z|-log(t/2)<=1/(8t²) and |Re(1/(2z))|<=1/(2t²). Their sum is <2/t². The sharp archimedean correction is 2 int_1^infty k(s) cos(ts)ds, bounded by 4k(1)/t<4/t by integration by parts and monotonicity. The pole correction for A is 4 int_0^1 cosh(s/2)cos(ts)ds, bounded by 4(2cosh(1/2)-1)/t<6/t. T lacks the latter. No unverified numerical symbol constant is assumed.

For N>=8, f(x)=log(x/4)/x² is nonnegative decreasing on [N,infinity), so the constant-frequency sum differs from its integral (log(N/4)+1)/N by at most f(N). For any nonzero angle theta modulo 2pi, geometric partial sums are bounded by 1/|sin(theta/2)|; Abel summation therefore bounds the corresponding oscillatory sum with positive decreasing weight by this constant times its first weight. For theta0, thetap, theta0-thetap, theta0+thetap we can take 2,3,6,2 respectively. These follow, for example, from 3.14<pi<3.15, .69<log 2<.70, and sin x>=x-x³/6 on [0,pi/2]. Hence the log-frequency error, including integral comparison, is <=3 log(N/4)/N². The prime cosine times (1-cos(n theta0)) splits by the product-to-sum identity; since 2b<1 its tail is bounded by (3+(6+2)/2)/N²=7/N². The symbol remainder contributes at most
    2 sum_(n>N) [8/(pi² n^4)+20/(pi n³)]
      <= 16/(3pi² N³)+20/(pi N²)
      <= 1/N³+7/N².
Altogether the following is a certified analytic bound for both operators:
    |R_N - (4/pi²)(log(N/4)+1)/N|
       <= (4/pi²)(3 log(N/4)+14+1/N)/N².                  (1)
