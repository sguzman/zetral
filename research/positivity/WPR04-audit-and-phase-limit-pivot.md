## 1. Audit of weighted rigidity and coherence

WPR01 uses the exact weighted cross-edge measure with coefficient exp(-2a)Lambda(n), because the product of the two exponential boundary weights cancels n^(-1/2) in the normalized prime shift. Both marginals are h(t)^2 ell_a(t)dt, with ell_a=(psi(X)-psi(X exp(-a)))/X, X=exp(2a-t), almost everywhere. The two-sided uniform row bound follows since X>=exp(a), the upper sum is PNT-controlled and the omitted lower sum is O(X exp(-a)). The edge-square expansion (3) gives the precise cross-form deficit. The transition average of a fixed bounded continuous observable converges uniformly in the other endpoint coordinate by PNT interval counts, compact step approximation and Chebyshev tail bounds; it does not assert total variation convergence of discrete measures.

The weighted Cauchy-Schwarz transfer estimate (7) uses both row marginals and the uniform transition limit. In a deep ordinary spectral subspace the normalized pole is uniformly negligible by the weak-null boundary estimate of PBC03 and its rank-two norm limit, while the same-half prime blocks vanish. This gives the nonnegative edge-energy/log-energy budget at depths >=c exp(a). For arbitrary unit form trials saturating the coefficient one, the normalized residual expectation tends to one and the archimedean expectation divided by exp(a) tends to zero. Its bounded self-adjoint norm tends to one, so the trials are approximate eigenvectors of that normalized residual. The strong-null residual limit implies weak-null boundary coordinates, which in turn makes the pole negligible. The edge energy tends to zero and the transfer identity forces both endpoint intensities to converge weakly to (1/2)exp(-t)dt. This is an intensity statement, not strong convergence of wavefunctions.

WPR02 compares two reflections on the support of a fixed local cutoff: for r,r' in [4,4+ell], ell<=1/10, the cutoff and its translate by r'-r are contained in (2,3); the common left coordinate r-s and both reflected right coordinates are valid for large a. Both local squared differences are bounded by the corresponding edge-energy terms, and averaging divides by the interval's prime log mass. The cutoff commutator costs O(ell^2) from the locally bounded unweighted ratio norm. Plancherel gives the exact averaged translation multiplier 2(1-|Phi|^2). The local cutoff norm has a nonzero limit from the intensity theorem. Choosing ell=max(E^(1/3),eta^(1/4),1/a) yields comparable mass by uniform PNT endpoint estimates and E/ell+ell^2->0. The band-restricted residual norm and approximate residual eigenvector identity give the needed low-frequency escape for form trials as well as eigenvectors. The fixed compact smooth multiplier shifts this to a smaller growing band by Young's inequality. The upper frequency cutoff follows from its o(exp(a)) log-energy bound. The weighted deficit estimate thus selects an actual frequency with coherence tending to one, |omega|ell->infinity and log(2+|omega|)=o(exp(a)). Proper prime powers have O(sqrt X) log mass, negligible compared with X ell. The result is conditional on saturation and neither assumes phase independence nor infers coherence from weak convergence.

WPR03's multinomial moment bound follows from unique factorization; the elementary off-diagonal integration estimate and integer-offset Cauchy-Schwarz give the displayed length term. Its inability to beat the persistent peak width is a limitation of that particular upper bound, not a lower bound on the true moment. The related Guth–Maynard source was actually inspected in its introductory HTML excerpt; no assertion that the paper addresses every possible coherence-exclusion method. The lattice-quadrature countermodel uses an a-dependent grid, not actual prime locations. Its geometric summatory error, weighted Schur estimate and rank-two strong limit obey the untwisted assumptions; exactly commensurable shifts admit modulation with log cost O(a). The fixed smooth endpoint trials then approach the coarse coefficient one after taking the supremum over their envelopes. This is a legitimate countermodel to untwisted reasoning, not a zeta counterexample.

## 2. Disposition: the sharper phase-limit question

Campaign XXVI produced a concrete necessary arithmetic coherence condition for saturation of the sharp coarse coefficient, and demonstrated why untwisted PNT and the elementary high-moment estimate alone do not establish its exclusion. A new direct question may give a fuller characterization rather than merely strengthen necessary conditions near the coefficient one.

For arbitrary expanding windows a and carriers omega, conjugate the physical prime operator by exp(i omega x). Its scaled endpoint cross block is the reflection integral against the twisted measure
    mu_(a,omega)=sum_n exp(-a)Lambda(n)/sqrt(n)
                       exp(i omega log n) delta_(2a-log n).
The untwisted positive measures converge to exp(-r/2)dr; thus any vague subsequential twisted limit should have density phi(r) exp(-r/2) with |phi|<=1. It induces a compact half-line Hankel kernel exp(-(t+s)/2) phi(t+s). For unbounded carriers whose logarithmic cost divided by exp(a) tends to tau, the pole should vanish on fixed smooth demodulated boundary envelopes while the archimedean contribution tends to tau. This suggests a lower bound for the ordinary normalized negative depth in terms of the Hankel norm minus tau.

A potential converse uses the exact compact-support frequency partition FES01 and packet moment estimate FES02 (both files read during this turn). Improve the fixed half-depth loss in FES02 by using a small adjustable moment penalty, for sequences with N(a)/exp(a) bounded away from zero and fixed support expansion h. The normalized selected packet should retain (1-o_h(1)) of the depth and have a uniformly bounded demodulated Fourier moment for each fixed penalty/h. Extract its translated endpoint envelopes strongly locally by compactness, use the uniform endpoint tail norm of the arithmetic shifts, and pass to a twisted measure limit. Let the fixed support expansion and penalty decrease only after the subsequential bounds are proved. If successful, this may give an exact variational characterization of limsup exp(-a)N_1(a) as the supremum over attainable twisted measure limits of a compact Hankel norm minus carrier cost. This is a proposed theorem, NOT proved in WPR04. In particular the converse requires treatment of mass escaping the endpoints, bounded carriers, and norm versus strong limits; do not presume those steps.
