The exponential correction is bounded in absolute value by E_h/omega^2. Thus |A(n)| <= sqrt(2)/2+2sqrt(2)h(1+E_h)/(pi^2(M+1)), as used in BC03. With decreasing nonnegative w_n on a parity progression, partial sums of e^(in theta) have modulus <=1/|sin theta|; the Abel sum bound uses total successive weight differences equal to the first weight.
