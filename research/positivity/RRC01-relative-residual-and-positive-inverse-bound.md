# RRC01 — Relative residual Gram and positive inverse-error comparison

2026-11-25. AI-assisted candidate deduction without RH. Uses ECR01, PBCI01, DSC01 and PMW04. Classical congruence and completion-of-squares inequalities; no independent review or priority claim.

Let S=A-R*C^-1R be the exact Schur matrix, C>=G>0 on the complementary space, and X any finite rational trial. Set U=A-R*X-X*R+X*CX and F=R-CX. Then S=U-F*C^-1F exactly. If an exact rational lower matrix U0<=U is positive definite, and H0>=F*G^-1F, the sufficient positivity test is H0<U0. Equivalently the largest generalized eigenvalue of (H0,U0) is <1. The comparison is relative to the actual upper-form energies, so it can identify a residual correlation obstruction invisible to individual positive diagonal tests. The generalized eigenvalue need not be numerically computed to certify it: for a rational invertible V, verify V*(U0-H0)V positive using exact integer leading principal minors. A failed sufficient inequality does not establish an actual negative direction.

## Rational energy scaling and diagnostics

Choose a rational basis for the low space, using the approximate upper matrix or the exact integer lower comparison to suggest near-null directions. For each direction i with a strictly positive estimated energy, choose a positive rational scale d_i so that d_i² U_ii is approximately one. For example select a power of two by comparing exact positive rational endpoints, which avoids an irrational square root and does not require exact normalization. Scale every trial coefficient by the corresponding d_i, and recompute actual U, residual rows, S0,S2,V2 and remainder bounds with the scaled rational coefficients. Invertibility of the basis and positivity of the final lower matrix remain separate exact tests. For extreme scales it is preferable to keep all trial coefficients as exact rationals or increase the fixed-point scale before rounding. A certificate may equally use unscaled exact integer matrices; scaling is primarily a diagnostic and a way to allocate numerical errors.

For fixed trial X and transformed columns, compare U, finite weighted residual Gram H_M, leading/second/third rank-one tail matrices, and fourth directional remainder in the energy-scaled coordinates. Record their signed entries and exact enclosure errors separately. The sum of their upper bounds is a positive residual majorant. An approximate dominant generalized eigenvector can suggest a direction for further trial improvement, but its sign or eigenvalue is not itself a certificate. For example an exact rational test direction with positive upper energy and lower residual comparison exceeding it only demonstrates failure of that comparison, not actual negative spectrum. If the upper energy itself is strictly negative after exact verification, the distinction in ECR01 applies.

## Retaining positive high corrections in the residual inverse

The global diagonal G loses information about K and the prime shift geometry. Let E be the complementary projection and C>=G+V D V* with D positive definite, for instance a finite positive column compression of the boundary K or Z=cI-P of LPE01 and FBC01. For arbitrary finite coefficient trial Y,
    F*C^-1F <= (F-VY)*G^-1(F-VY)+Y*D^-1Y.
This is precisely the variational inequality of PBCI01 applied to the remaining residual F rather than to the original low-to-high cross R. The exact minimizer for the positive lower comparison is Y=(D^-1+V*G^-1V)^-1 V*G^-1F; a numerical solve need not be trusted since any rational trial is admissible. If V=E K W, D=(W*KW)^-1, the penalty is Y*(W*KW)Y, so no verified inversion is required in evaluating the cost. Likewise for the positive prime correction. Positive even pole columns can be included. The low/cross/high exact form Gram U is unchanged. This correction may be much more effective when F is already small than when approximating the whole R; no numerical improvement is asserted yet.

The tail of F-VY is still a signed linear combination of the actual boundary and prime columns and the finite-support trial coefficients. For selected W indices below the evaluation cutoff, use RMT01's common rational expansion for each group of signed coefficients; if different groups no longer share one S0 or S2, retain each group as its own rank-one row and bound cross terms by weighted Cauchy. The pole remains exactly computable. No dropped finite high residual is allowed.

## Adaptive high support

A second legitimate improvement is to enlarge the finite support of X rather than the evaluation cutoff alone. ECR01's graph-core argument guarantees convergence of the residual comparison for each fixed strictly positive S and coercive C, but not useful uniform rates. At PMW03 the main even obstruction is the correlated finite residual Gram plus tiny near-null energies. A bounded larger T test, with enough rate precision and tail cutoff to control the increased moment constants, is justified. Preserve full relative diagnostics before deciding whether to add the positive inverse correction. No claim that this will certify larger windows. RRC01 complete as candidate.
