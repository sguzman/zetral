Implement PMW01 with exact reduced rational breakpoints, integer cross-product comparison, midpoint evaluation and integer upper weights (491,635,347,720)/1000 for m=2,3,4,5. The actual log/sqrt weight intervals are checked using the RMT02 10^30 lattice and integer square-root bounds. The separate tower fallback <2.25 is checked by squaring the positive 0.89 bound for the 2-tower and adding the other two weights.

Start with constant integer weight 10^9. At each of 32 steps compute (1000 I+1000 P_beta)w on the exact refined rational partition, rescale its maximum to 10^9, round to nearest integer with positive floor 1, merge equal adjacent values, and verify the actual resulting witness. The iteration is merely a heuristic; every accepted upper bound is calculated independently by integer ceiling of (1000 P_beta w)/w on all refined cells. No floating arithmetic is used in the search or verification. The best witness is retained and checked again.

Actual bound sequence at steps 0,1,2,5,10,20,31 is 2.193,1.717,1.616,1.578,1.559,1.550,1.547. The best first occurs at step 31, with 437 positive rational-partition cells, 495 verification cells, and worst verification interval (1,32805/32768). Every inequality is exact, and the full 438 endpoints, 437 positive integer weights, actual upper weight enclosures and search history are preserved in the JSON. Step 32 ties the upper bound with a larger partition, so step 31 is retained.

By PMW01 the actual combined prime operator on physical length log6 has norm <=1.547, hence also on every shorter window. This improves the 2.25 separate-tower fallback by approximately 31.2%, and the direct constant-weight bound 2.193 by approximately 29.5%. This is an upper bound only; it is not claimed to be the exact norm or an optimal witness. It does not assume finite orbit components or extrapolate beyond log6. Prime 5 is included with Lambda(5)/sqrt5; 6 has zero von Mangoldt weight. No positivity certificate or RH proof is implied by the norm witness alone.
