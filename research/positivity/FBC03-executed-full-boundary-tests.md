## Method

Use FBC01–02 and LPE01–02 for h in the one-prime regime. The entire infinite boundary correction is represented in the low/cross entries and selected positive complementary columns via F_eta scalar sums; no finite-rate lower model is substituted. The scalar sum enclosures use J=192 and midpoint-integral errors at R=383.5. Diagonal entries use the direct positive series and integral tail; off-diagonal entries use divided differences with exact interval arithmetic. Full low digamma uses its integral tail with midpoint error, and gamma uses Jameson (2019), equation (1), DOI 10.1186/s13660-019-2104-9. The atan argument is reduced by inversion or a pi/4 shift, then enclosed by 30 Taylor terms with symmetric next-term error; exp, log, pi and trigonometric values inherit the explicitly bounded prior series. Each fixed-point operation is outward rounded at scale 10^18.

Keep the full prime matrix and exact pole. Select full boundary and prime positive columns W_b,W_p containing the low modes. The high comparison is the diagonal lower bound plus these two positive column compressions and optional even pole. The penalties are the exact full boundary Gram, exact prime Gram, and h/2 for the scaled positive even pole. A floating ridge-regularized Cholesky solve chooses an arbitrary trial, rounded to denominator 10^12; it is NOT used as a certified inverse. Verify the complete variational cost, finite weighted residual Gram and analytic omitted tail by outward intervals. Subtract the tail upper endpoint and symmetric entry-radius row sum, then test interval LDL of the exact rational lower matrix.
