## 1. Finite exact moments cannot generally certify an interior capacity

Let mu be a finite positive discrete measure on [ell,infinity) with an exponential moment and with z>ell outside its support, at positive distance from it. Suppose it has at least N+1 distinct atoms x_0,...,x_N of positive mass. Choose such atoms. Let L_j(x) be the Lagrange cardinal polynomials for these nodes. For any real polynomial p of degree <=N, sum_j L_j(x)p(x_j)=p(x). For x close to z, define
    mu_x=mu+epsilon delta_x-epsilon sum_j L_j(x) delta_(x_j).
On a small fixed neighborhood of z all L_j are bounded, and epsilon>0 can be chosen fixed and sufficiently small that each altered mass at x_j stays positive. Choose the neighborhood to avoid the original support and stay above ell. The measure mu_x is positive, retains the exponential moment property, and has exactly the same moments of degrees 0,...,N as mu. Its resolvent at z is
    m_x(z)=m(z)+epsilon/(x-z)-epsilon sum_j L_j(x)/(x_j-z).
The last sum is uniformly bounded as x approaches z from either side. Hence these exact finite data admit capacities tending to either +infinity or -infinity. The perturbed measures still have discrete locally finite support when mu does. They can be realized as diagonal compact-resolvent operators with a vector having the designated squared coordinates (append uncoupled eigenvalues tending to +infinity if the support is finite). This is an abstract obstruction, not a modification of the actual zeta operator. It also does not preserve any externally certified gap about z or necessarily preserve the negative eigenvalue count. If the actual pole measure is supported on fewer than N+1 points, the construction may not apply; do not assert infinite support without proof.

This shows why APM03's convergent exterior enclosures cannot be continued through the spectrum using finite moments alone. For an even-sector positivity test at z=0 with a negative eigenvalue, independent information on spectral separation is essential. Infinite exact data do determine the capacity by APM01–03, so there is no contradiction. The obstruction concerns finite data with only the indicated lower support and exponential integrability, not all arithmetic information.
