# Campaign I — spectral convergence

Goal: reconstruct and investigate the spectral approximation to Riemann zeros associated with Suzuki's Weil quadratic form, including recent RH work insofar as relevant.

Ordered milestones:
- B01: establish repository charter, state, scoped queue and source seed.
- B02: resolve Situs location and establish situated ontology references; verify canonical persistence.
- B03: configure and verify minimal CI and Pages; introduce pinned Lean, Rust, and reproducibility checks when corresponding artifacts exist.
- R01: map foundational and current literature; verify precise statements, assumptions, prior art and source versions.
- R02: reconstruct Suzuki's construction and proof of convergence in human-readable form; document exact domains, boundary conditions and multiplicity limitations.
- R03: reproduce published numerical examples independently; document arithmetic input, precision, stability and reproducibility.
- R04: specify quantitative convergence questions; prior-art check, derive bounds or identify obstructions, test counterexamples.
- R05: pursue the bounded convergence investigation using explicit hypotheses and reproducible evidence; verify any claimed results where feasible.
- R06: consolidate results, unresolved questions, proof audits, and public exposition. Stop.

Acceptance: B01–B03 and R01–R06 have durable evidence or explicit blocker dispositions; original theorem not required. Negative findings are valid outcomes. Any broader spectral/dynamical investigation requires a new approved campaign. No theorem is claimed at bootstrap.
