# V02 — Targeted novelty and prior-art assessment

Date: 2026-11-25 UTC. Baseline: candidate v0.1, blob 6fcdb4c0a3cfe8de0a2986ff04f05a3c20c20952. This is a targeted literature assessment, not an exhaustive bibliographic or peer review. No priority claim is made.

## The claim being compared

Under RH and Suzuki's sampling and compression theorems, for fixed n and sufficiently large a, the positive eigenvalue t_(a,n) of the exact phase-pi differentiation extension satisfies
    0 <= t_(a,n)-gamma_n <= K_n X^(5/2) exp(-2 pi X), X=exp(2a).
The limiting ordinates are distinct, with no assumed simplicity. The proof uses normalized Fourier samples of divided-out xi kernels, smooth compact truncation, and a quadratic Ritz bound. In particular this is NOT a statement about the lowest L2 Rayleigh quotient of the localized Weil quadratic form, the zeros of its ground-state transform, or the eigenvalues of a finite Fourier matrix. Claims with different operators cannot be ranked by comparing their exponents alone.

## Direct antecedents (primary source sections inspected)

1. M. Suzuki, Spectral convergence associated with Weil's quadratic form, arXiv:2610.32704v1, 23 Oct 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2610.32704v1. Introduction, Thm 1.1, §2.3, Thm 3.3 and §4: exact same target operator and compression; monotone fixed-index convergence from strong projection convergence, Hilbert–Schmidt approximation and max–min. The inspected proof does not provide an explicit fixed-index convergence rate. Theorems and definitions are imported; no claim that Zetral constructs the operator independently. Search within this version for 'rate' returned no matches, which by itself is not a novelty test.
2. M. Suzuki, On the Hilbert space derived from the Weil distribution, published online 3 Nov 2025, DOI https://doi.org/10.4153/S0008414X25101739, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/canadian-journal-of-mathematics/article/on-the-hilbert-space-derived-from-the-weil-distribution/FD33EBD117A6B448053450DD8D62ADB6 . Sections 2.3, 5 and 6: the global model has an orthogonal basis of eigenvectors indexed by distinct ordinates. The de Branges basis uses divided entire functions S_theta(z)/(z-gamma); the paper also constructs normalized sampled representatives with multiplicity weights. Consequently neither the coordinate isolation idea nor the global Hilbert space is new. Important distinction: the global model-space representatives need not have the Fourier decay of our xi quotient witness. The specific compact truncation estimate for that witness and its transfer to the localized differentiation eigenvalues is the narrow point under assessment.
3. A. Bondarenko, D. Radchenko, K. Seip, Fourier Interpolation with Zeros of Zeta and L-Functions, Constructive Approximation 57 (2023), 405–461, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s00365-022-09599-w . Theorem 1.1, Corollary 1.1: interpolation involving values at zeros (including derivatives through multiplicity minus one) and Fourier samples at log(n)/(4 pi) for even functions in a strip-analytic class. This is stronger interpolation machinery with a different objective; does not itself give a rate for Suzuki's exact localized spectrum. Cite as established interpolation context, not as the source of our bound.
4. A. V. Knyazev and M. E. Argentati, Rayleigh–Ritz Majorization Error Bounds with Applications to FEM, SIAM J. Matrix Anal. Appl. 31 (2010), DOI https://doi.org/10.1137/08072574X, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0701784 ; abstract and related 2008 article DOI https://doi.org/10.1137/070684628 inspected. Squared principal-angle bounds for invariant trial subspaces are established approximation theory. Our elementary leading-subspace estimate is an application/special case, not an original general Ritz theorem.

## Closest rapid-window-decay literature

5. X. Zhu, Weil positivity in compact windows, arXiv:2608.24827v2, 2 Sep 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2608.24827v2 . Thm 1.3 and §13: under RH the least Weil-form quotient lambda*(L) obeys lambda*(L)<=exp(-L exp(L)) for sufficiently large L. The trial function uses a truncated zero product and sinc window. The paper distinguishes the proved upper bound from its empirical Landau–Widom law at scale exp(2L). Thm 1.1 gives a finite lower reduction for the form, useful as possible V05 background but not a certified differentiation eigenvalue bound without further arguments.
6. Ryota Mori, Unconditional doubly exponential upper bounds for windowed Weil quadratic forms, https://github.com/moriryota/weil-window-upper-bounds . README release/version discussion and theorem statements accessed directly, full paper and Lean build not inspected. For mu=lambda^2>=5, reported Theorem A is lambda_min(lambda)<=1.563e9 mu^8 exp(-2 pi mu); Theorem B is <=1.362e9 mu^23 exp(-4 pi mu), using Kaiser–Bessel windows. These concern the bottom of the form on the multiplicative window [lambda^-1,lambda], with mu=exp(2a) when a=log lambda. This is substantial overlap in theta-window decay and the same exponential scale in A; B is faster for that different quantity. The README reports Lean verification only for the existential test-function form estimate of A, not its spectral Rayleigh transfer or B. Do not treat these as independently verified by Zetral. It would be misleading to call our decay rate the first doubly exponential Weil-window result, or to claim ours improves Mori's result.
7. A. Connes, C. Consani, H. Moscovici, Zeta Spectral Triples, arXiv:2511.22755v1, 27 Nov 2025, https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22755v1 . Thm 1.1: rank-one perturbed scaling operator and its spectral determinant from the transform of the least form eigenvector, assuming simplicity/evenness of that finite ground state. §§6–7: numerical tracking and prolate comparison. This is a different construction from Suzuki's inverse compression. Connes's survey arXiv:2602.04022 (3 Feb 2026) was located; its full content not audited here.
8. T. Kim et al., A Numerical Realization of Suzuki's Weil-Quadratic-Form Operator, arXiv:2607.24830v3, 28 Sep 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2607.24830v3 . Previously unavailable revision now accessible; the earlier August date for v3 was erroneous. §1.3, §§3.8–3.9: authors distinguish A_a from differentiation extensions; R7 concerns rapid decrease of the lowest A_a eigenvalue, R8 concerns numerical characteristic functions and a generic-phase zero density observation. Does not establish our fixed-index bound for the specific zero-shift phase-pi extension in the October paper. Numerical claims not adopted as rigorous certificates.
