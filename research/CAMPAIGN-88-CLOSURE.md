## Scope and unresolved barriers

The shared endpoint limit does not give a finite-parameter intertwiner,
equality or transfer of positive-parameter zero sets, norm-resolvent
convergence, uniformity in the spectral parameter, or convergence of Suzuki's
original arithmetic Hamiltonians. The Clark model is RH-conditional and
zero-divisor based; no zero-independent Hilbert–Pólya construction follows.
General operator intertwiners remain open. The exact multiplier obstruction
and positive-mixture no-go are scoped results, not a comprehensive
impossibility theorem.
