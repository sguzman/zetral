# Campaign LXXXV closure — Hermite sampling and arithmetic compression

Date: 2026-11-27. Disposition: closed within the bounded direct-bridge
investigation; the general multiplicity-sensitive Hilbert–Pólya program stays
open.

## Findings

1. Goncalves–Littmann's standard higher-jet theorem cannot be applied to all
   distinct zeta ordinates: its hypotheses imply uniform Euclidean separation,
   contradicted by Selberg's `cT log T` lower bound for distinct critical-line
   ordinates. This is route-specific, not a no-go for nonuniform jets
   (`research/operators/B3-05-separation-obstruction-to-zeta-hermite-sampling.md`).
2. Marzo–Nitzan–Olsen's doubling-phase theorem is for value samples. Its
   uniform interval density hypotheses are not supplied by the centered
   Riemann–von Mangoldt asymptotic; multiplicity-counted density and the
   distinct-ordinate node set differ. Under RH plus simplicity, the centered
   comparison is exactly at the theorem's critical threshold, not the strict
   sufficient range (`research/operators/B3-06-doubling-phase-density-gap.md`).
3. Equivalent renormings of the existing Weil completion cannot fix the
   unbounded derivative-sample functional (B3-04). A genuinely changed
   completion still requires its own proved sampling map, arithmetic local
   forms, domains, and inverse-compression identity.

## Verification boundary

The findings are source-based theorem-hypothesis checks and an elementary
dual-norm argument, not numerical or formal results. We do not claim that
weighted/nonuniform Hermite sampling is impossible, that zeta zeros are simple,
or that no other arithmetic compression can exist. Primary sources and exact
limitations are recorded in the B3-04/05/06 notes. No novelty claim is made.

## Pivot

Campaign LXXXVI investigates a qualitatively different operator route already
present in the literature: shifted-xi canonical systems. The first question
is whether their positive-shift family has a rigorously controlled operator
limit at the degenerate critical endpoint, with a zeta spectral correspondence
and an arithmetic compression identity.
