# B3-56 — A coercive-block bound for negative trace

Date: 2026-11-28. Status: exact finite-dimensional Schur-complement
criterion; application to the Weil form remains conditional and open.

## Question

After localized negative trace fails to be subadditive (B3-55), can a
coercive high-frequency block control the negative trace generated by the
cross-frequency coupling?

## Theorem

Let `L,H` be finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces and let

    T = [[A, B*], [B, D]]

be self-adjoint on `L direct_sum H`, with `D >= delta I` for some `delta>0`.
Then

    Tr(T_-) <= Tr(A_-) + Tr(B* D^(-1) B)
             = Tr(A_-) + ||D^(-1/2) B||_HS^2.

In particular, for the low-frequency projection `P_L`,

    Tr(P_L T_- P_L) <= Tr(A_-) + ||D^(-1/2)B||_HS^2.

The same upper bound holds for any positive contraction `0<=M<=I` in place
of `P_L`, since `Tr(M T_-)<=Tr(T_-)`.

If the low block `A` is positive, only the cross-block term remains.

## Proof

Take an orthonormal eigenbasis `psi_j=(x_j,y_j)` of the negative spectral
subspace of `T`, with eigenvalues `lambda_j<0`. Completing the square gives

    <T psi_j,psi_j>
      = ||D^(1/2)y_j + D^(-1/2)B x_j||^2
        + <(A-B*D^(-1)B)x_j,x_j>.

Consequently

    -lambda_j <= <(A_- + B*D^(-1)B)x_j,x_j>.

The low components of an orthonormal family satisfy
`sum_j |x_j><x_j| <= I_L`, because they are the projection of an
orthonormal family in `L direct_sum H`. Summing the previous inequality
therefore yields

    Tr(T_-)=sum_j(-lambda_j)
      <= Tr(A_- + B*D^(-1)B).

The localized bound follows from `0<=P_L<=I` and `T_-≥ 0`.

## Falsification check and relevance

The B3-55 counterexample does not contradict this theorem: with low space
`span{u}` and high space `span{e2,e3}`, its high block is
`D=diag(-1,-1)`, so the coercivity hypothesis fails. This identifies what
additional structure a summation argument must establish; no generic
subadditivity is recovered.

For a frequency decomposition of a preconditioned Weil form, the theorem
would turn a positive high-frequency block plus a weighted Hilbert–Schmidt
bound on the low/high coupling into control of the full negative trace. The
actual ACG semilocal tail operator is not known here to have such a positive
high block; proving that fact without assuming H2′ could be circular. ACG's
§8.6 already performs a conditional finite Schur reduction under H2′. This
lemma supplies a separate abstract sufficient criterion, not a new proof of
that source result. See Andrews, Connes, La Gatta, arXiv:2611.13129v1,
§8.6, especially Theorem 8.14: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13129.

The finite-dimensional theorem is fully proved above. An infinite-dimensional
version would require domain control, a coercive self-adjoint high block,
trace-class `A_-` and `D^(-1/2)B`, and justification of spectral truncation;
none is claimed. In particular this does not prove H2′, positivity, a
Hilbert–Pólya correspondence, or RH. No novelty claim is made; the Schur
complement mechanism is standard, and the recorded contribution is the
explicit trace bound and its required hypotheses for this research route.
