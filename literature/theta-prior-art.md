# R05 — theta localization prior art

Accessed 2026-11-24 Mexico. Scope: the exact phase-pi differentiation spectrum of Suzuki, not all Weil-form or prolate approximations.

- Suzuki, https://arxiv.org/html/2610.32704v1, §2.3 sampling and §3.3 inverse compression, Thm 1.1 / §4: re-read primary text, exact local operator spectrum and qualitative monotone limit. The specific fixed-index theta-cardinal bound in research/theta-cardinal-bound.md is not stated in these sections.
- Lagarias and Montague, The Integral of the Riemann xi-Function, https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.4348, (1.1)-(1.2), classical even theta Fourier kernel. Exact series cross-checked against the indexed journal version https://rikkyo.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/8642/files/AA00610867_60-01%2C02_14.pdf (search excerpt only; PDF not reviewed). Romik, https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.06330, related classical representation. The kernel and its rapid decay are not new.
- Bondarenko, Radchenko, Seip, Fourier Interpolation with Zeros of Zeta and L-Functions, https://doi.org/10.1007/s00365-022-09599-w, related cardinal/interpolation ideas. Targeted source search; not a comprehensive theorem comparison.
- Zhu, Weil positivity in compact windows, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24827v2, conditional construction using canonical product and sinc for the least Weil-form eigenvalue. Related polynomial localization is prior art.
- Mori, Unconditional doubly exponential upper bounds for the bottom of windowed Weil quadratic forms, https://github.com/moriryota/weil-window-upper-bounds, public repository summary inspected: theta cutoff gives estimates with exponential scale exp(-2 pi lambda^2), some formalization reported. Not independently built; concerns the bottom of the windowed Weil form, not Suzuki's differentiation extension.
- bramble_trace, Quantitative theta trial spaces for the Weil form, https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/25/v1, indexed abstract/summary: theta derivatives and fixed-index window eigenvalues of the form operator. Unreviewed platform lead, not relied on as a proof. Related https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/80/v2 and /papers/81/v1 explicitly separate Suzuki's qualitative eigenvalue convergence from bottom-of-form or ground-state-transform tracking estimates. The former cites theta cutoff at scale exp(-2 pi exp(2L)); the latter reports a stronger bottom-of-form estimate at another scale. These are substantial overlap in technique and must be acknowledged.

Conclusion: both theta truncation and polynomial/sinc localization are known techniques. The specific proposed application here is to normalized cardinal vectors obtained by dividing xi by the exact multiplicity at one target ordinate, followed by squared Ritz projection-defect control for Suzuki's inverse compression. The targeted searches did not identify this exact bound for this exact operator. This is insufficient to claim originality, priority, or publishable novelty. A human expert or independent reviewer should compare the full relevant papers before submission. No claim of independent Lean verification.
